## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the institutional authority, technical precision, and enduring legacy of an African renewable energy pioneer operating since 1982. The visual language bridges heavy-infrastructure execution with contemporary digital publishing: structural clarity, geometric discipline, and generous negative space evoke engineered durability rather than speculative technology.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Infrastructure Editorial**: Combines the precision of architectural blueprints, the dignity of public institutions, and the legibility of global energy publications.
- **Architectural Rhythm**: Crisp 1px hairline borders (`#E5E7EB`) define distinct zones, structural grids, and technical cards without visual clutter.
- **Material Restraint**: Surfaces are predominantly crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with alternating muted architectural bands (`#F6F7F9`) and authoritative dark charcoal focal zones (`#111827`). 
- **Subsidiary Presence**: The vibrant red (`#E03A3E`) acts as a deliberate accentâ€”used strictly for subsidiary highlights, active states, and critical technical calls to actionâ€”anchored by Holdings Charcoal (`#2B303A`).

### Operating Pillar Architecture
The UI reflects a group structure consisting of four operating companies, each mapped to a shared corporate spine:
1. **Industries**: The foundation (EPC, distribution, engineering utility).
2. **Energy (SEPCO)**: The commercial utility layer (energy trading, Virtual Power Plants, grid services).
3. **Maintenance**: Operational longevity (asset monitoring, preventive maintenance, uptime).
4. **Carbon**: The environmental commodity arm (I-RECs, carbon offset integrity, certification).

## Colors

The palette balances deep carbon tones with high-contrast functional neutrals, enriched by a disciplined heritage red.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Brand Red (`#E03A3E`)**: Expresses kinetic power, technical dynamism, and subsidiary visibility. Used sparingly for interactive triggers, active tab indicators, and technical metrics.
- **Deep Red AAA (`#99151B`)**: Used for hover states on primary interactive elements and for accessible colored typography on light surfaces.
- **Holdings Charcoal (`#2B303A`)**: The primary anchor for group-level badges, technical headers, navigation chips, and institutional governance sections.
- **Ink Charcoal (`#111827`)**: The definitive dark surface. Used for the global footer, contrast statement panels, and high-priority headline typography.
- **Body Neutral (`#374151`)**: Optimized for sustained reading across technical whitepapers, case studies, and asset briefs.
- **Muted Slate & Captions (`#4B5563` & `#6B7280`)**: Secondary metadata, technical spec labels, and subsidiary subtext.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary page canvas and card background.
- **Surface Contrast Tier (`#F6F7F9`)**: Alternating technical bands, parameter panels, and data table headers.
- **Structural Border (`#E5E7EB`)**: Precise 1px hairline divider across all modular interfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear informational cadence through three distinct type families:

1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headings)**: Provides a sharp, engineered geometry that mirrors infrastructure drafts and technical power distribution diagrams. Use uppercase tracking (`+0.05em`) strictly for eyebrow headers and category identifiers.
2. **Inter (Narrative & Body)**: Offers institutional legibility across long-form case studies, environmental governance statements, and operational descriptions. High optical balance ensures crisp readability against neutral surface bands.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Metadata, Operational Metrics & Timestamps)**: Anchors historical markers (e.g., `EST. 1982`), grid performance indicators (MWh, MWp, COâ‚‚e), asset tracking IDs, and certification standards (e.g., `I-REC STANDARD v2.1`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on an authoritative 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum content container width of 1440px to retain structural integrity on wide workstations.

### Grid & Alignment Rules
- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px â€“ 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) page margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) page margins.
- **Vertical Cadence**: High-level section separators utilize generous breathing room (`space-2xl` / `4rem` to `6rem`) to convey confidence and corporate scale. Internal component stacking follows strict 8px increments.
- **Alternating Modular Bands**: Sections cycle between white (`#FFFFFF`) and architectural grey (`#F6F7F9`) to clearly divide group functions without needing heavy visual separators. Full-bleed dark bands (`#111827`) are reserved for the infrastructure footprint, regional presence map, and footer.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the tactile seriousness of an engineering and energy concern, this design system avoids floating cards, glossy blurs, and pronounced drop shadows. Depth is achieved entirely through planar boundaries and crisp tonal contrast.

### Structural Depth Techniques
- **Zero Drop-Shadow Baseline**: Elevation is articulated through hairline borders (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) coupled with tonal shifts between card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) and parent containers (`#F6F7F9`).
- **Precision Dark Inversion**: High-priority modules (e.g., Executive Summary, Grid Capacity Metrics) utilize dark ink surfaces (`#111827`) embedded directly into light layouts, producing visual gravitas without simulated vertical elevation.
- **Interactive State Elevation**: Interactive infrastructure cards do not lift vertically on hover; instead, they shift their border from neutral grey (`#E5E7EB`) to Holdings Charcoal (`#2B303A`) or Primary Red (`#E03A3E`) with an immediate, mechanical CSS transition (`150ms ease-out`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework is grounded in structured modernism:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied uniformly to cards, technical panels, interactive buttons, modal frames, and imagery viewports. This creates a restrained, modern posture that feels solid and engineered.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Reserved strictly for metadata tags, historical badges (e.g., `Est. 1982`), operational status chips (e.g., `Grid-Synchronized`), and subsidiary identity badges.
- **Hairline Precision**: All borders are strictly 1px in width. Double-thick borders, heavy drop-shadow contours, and skeuomorphic bevels are strictly forbidden.

## Components

- **Primary Group Action**: Dark charcoal background (`#2B303A`), crisp white text, 8px border-radius, 12px 24px padding. On hover, background shifts smoothly to `#111827`.
- **Subsidiary Action / Accent**: Primary red background (`#E03A3E`), white text. On hover, background transitions to AAA Dark Red (`#99151B`).
- **Secondary / Technical Outlined**: Transparent background, 1px solid `#E5E7EB` border, ink charcoal text (`#111827`). Hover state fills background with `#F6F7F9` and darkens border to `#2B303A`.
- **Text Link with Technical Marker**: Body font with an adjacent directional glyph (`â†’`), colored in `#2B303A` or `#E03A3E`, retaining clear hover color shifts.
- *Constraint*: No retail or e-commerce action copy (e.g., "Add to Cart", "Buy Now", "Shop Panels"). Actions must reflect corporate and stakeholder engagement (e.g., "Review Technical Brief", "Inquire Regarding PPA", "Contact Operating Entity").

### Badges, Status Chips & Subsidiary Indicators

- **Operating Division Chips**:
  - *Industries*: Neutral grey band with red indicator dot.
  - *Energy (SEPCO)*: Dark charcoal band with white typography.
  - *Maintenance*: Neutral grey band with status green dot (`#10B981`) for uptime monitoring.
  - *Carbon*: Neutral grey band with verification emerald indicator.
- **Specification Chip**: Light background (`#F6F7F9`), border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `JetBrains Mono` (`#4B5563`).

### Cards & Division Matrices
- **Structural Entity Cards**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, border-radius 8px, padding 32px. Features an eyebrow label in `JetBrains Mono`, headline in `Space Grotesk`, concise descriptive summary in `Inter`, and an operating scope checklist.
- **Metric Highlight Card**: Pure white canvas with subtle `#F6F7F9` header band; displays numerical metrics in `Space Grotesk` (`metric-num`) followed by a strict technical unit designation (e.g., `MWh GENERATED`, `ASSETS UNDER O&M`).

### Lists & Technical Tables
- **Specification Lists**: Border-bottom `1px solid #E5E7EB` between rows; label on the left in `#6B7280` (`Inter` 14px), value on the right in `#111827` (`JetBrains Mono` 14px, right-aligned).
- **Executive & Capability Rows**: Horizontal split layout featuring a 4-column overview title and an 8-column detail block separated by 1px vertical or horizontal grid rules.

### Input Fields & Inquiries
- **Corporate Inquiry Field**: White background, 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, 8px radius, 12px 16px padding. Text color `#111827`. Focus state triggers a clean 1px border highlight (`#2B303A`) without heavy outer halos.
- **Field Labels**: `Inter` 14px medium, color `#374151`, stacked 6px above inputs with optional monospace helper notes.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- **Technical Filter Segment**: Pill-shaped or 8px rounded segmented controls inside a `#F6F7F9` background tray. Active tab uses white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a clean 1px hairline border and subtle `#111827` text.
